wb-decide

Record a decision into ## Decisions — and review the ones a background sweep drafted, waiting on a per-task shelf, at /wb-save, /close-out and the weekly review.

current

Overview

/wb-decide is the "record" half of decision auto-capture. Decisions made in a wb session used to vanish unless someone remembered to write them into the task file's ## Decisions section. That matters because ## Decisions is what a later session, a reviewer, or you in a month reads to learn why the code looks the way it does.

The wb-capture plugin (claude/.claude/skills/wb-capture/, hooks only) closes the gap in the background. A model "sweep" reads the session's transcript, finds decisions nobody recorded, and drafts each one as its own small file on the task's shelf (a per-task folder of drafts waiting for your review). Nobody is nudged and nothing is injected into the agent's context. A draft just waits until you are already reviewing work: a /wb-save, a /close-out, or the weekly review. In between, a count of waiting drafts shows in the tmux status bar and on wb board. Hooks never write the task store; accepting a draft is an ordinary wb append … Decisions that the review step runs for you.

The lifecycle, in plain words:

  async sweep (Stop hook, after 4 quiet turns) ─┐
  checkpoint sweep (/wb-save, /close-out,       ├─> model reads the new turns,
                    /wb-decide shelved)        ─┘    your ## Decisions, and the shelf
                                                          │
                          one file per drafted decision   ▼
              ~/.local/state/wb/capture/shelved/<task>/<timestamp>-<session>.md
                          │                                │
        ✎N in the status bar,                              ▼
        DRAFTS column and chip on wb board       review at a checkpoint:
        (you can see it is waiting)              /wb-save · /close-out · /wb-decide shelved
                                                 ├─ accept / edit -> wb append Decisions, then
                                                 │                   accept: file moves to resolved/
                                                 ├─ dismiss       -> file moves to resolved/
                                                 └─ leave         -> stays on the shelf (logged)
                                                          │
                          weekly review: drafts older than 7 days, whatever the task's
                          status, plus any left at 3+ reviews (offered for dismissal)

Step by step:

  1. The sweep drafts. After the task file has gone 4 turns unchanged (the async sweep, an async Stop hook), or whenever a checkpoint asks for one (the checkpoint sweep), a headless model reads the new part of the transcript. It also sees three lists: your recorded ## Decisions, the drafts already waiting on the shelf, and the drafts you dismissed in the last 14 days, so a later turn that recaps a decision does not draft it twice or revive a rejected one. If it finds unrecorded decisions it writes up to 3 entries, and each entry is shelved as its own file (so "one draft" always means "one decision"). If it finds none it answers NONE and nothing is written.
  2. The count shows. The tmux status bar shows ✎N (peach) for the current session's task when N drafts are waiting, and nothing at zero or when the session has no task. wb board shows a DRAFTS column (text view) and a drafts chip beside the PR chip (HTML view).
  3. A checkpoint reviews. /wb-save, /close-out and /wb-decide shelved all run the same procedure ("step 2b" in the wb-decide skill): run one checkpoint sweep and wait for it, then show each of the task's drafts one at a time and ask accept, edit, dismiss or leave. Accepting records the entry into ## Decisions and moves the draft to resolved/. Dismissing moves it to resolved/. Leaving keeps it on the shelf and logs that this review showed it.
  4. The weekly review catches stragglers. /weekly-review lists every shelved draft older than seven days, whatever its task's status (so tasks that never close still get reviewed), together with drafts left behind by finished tasks. A draft you have seen and left at 3 or more reviews is offered for dismissal: it is probably not a real decision, or the review has become tiresome. The skill offers, it never dismisses unasked.

Nothing is ever deleted unseen. An accepted or dismissed draft is moved to ~/.local/state/wb/capture/resolved/<task>/, where the reaper keeps it for 30 days.

What changed (2026-10-24). The agent-facing nudge and phrase matching are gone. The log from the first five days showed why: of 38 nudges, 3 were genuinely followed by the agent, and phrase matches were nearly all recaps of old decisions. Every draft that was ever accepted was accepted by a person reviewing it (10 of 12 reviewed), so the drafts now go to you at moments you are reviewing, not to the agent in the middle of a request. The strikes, follow/ignore bookkeeping and the two-phase accept --begin went with the nudge.

Try it now

In any wb session, after something is actually settled:

/wb-decide

or /wb-decide <one-line hint> to say which decision. Claude reads the task's existing ## Decisions for style, composes decided / rejected / why, appends it with wb append, and confirms:

Recorded to ## Decisions.

To review what the sweep drafted, any time:

/wb-decide shelved

It runs a checkpoint sweep first (so turns not yet swept are covered), then walks the drafts. And to check the machinery:

/wb-decide status

Reference

Modes of /wb-decide

Invocation What it does
/wb-decide [hint] Compose a new entry from the conversation and wb append it under ## Decisions. If nothing was actually decided it says so and writes nothing. Shelved drafts are not part of this path.
/wb-decide shelved Run the checkpoint review: one checkpoint sweep, then each of this task's drafts one at a time: accept (optionally edited), dismiss, or leave. Ends with one line counting recorded / dismissed / left.
/wb-decide dismiss <path> Dismiss one shelved draft by path (moves it to resolved/). A bare dismiss with no path is a usage error.
/wb-decide mute / unmute Per-session mute flag: silences the async sweep and makes a checkpoint sweep print skipped: muted. Existing drafts can still be reviewed. Each is logged.
/wb-decide status Print mute state, the session id, the log path, and one shelved: <path> line per draft on this task's shelf ((stale) after a stale one; shelved: none). Read-only.

Entries are append-only; a correction is a new entry carrying a (supersedes: <date> "<title fragment>") line that names the one it replaces (the date plus a quoted substring of its title). The write uses a <<'WB_DECISION_END' heredoc rather than <<'EOF', so a draft line reading exactly EOF can't end it early.

Accepting is one step. Chain the append and the accept with &&, so a failed append skips the accept and the draft stays shelved:

wb append <task> Decisions - <<'WB_DECISION_END' && ~/.config/scripts/tmux/wb-capture.sh accept <path>
…the entry…
WB_DECISION_END

The checkpoint sweep. wb-capture.sh sweep runs the sweep in forced mode and waits for it (up to about two minutes: its child timeout is 60 seconds, doubled for its one stale rerun, about $0.12 at the default model). It ignores the four-quiet-turns rule and the failure backoff, because one attempt at a checkpoint is the point, and it skips the model call when nothing was written since the last sweep. It prints exactly one line and always exits 0, so a skill always goes on to the review:

Line Meaning
drafted N N drafts were shelved for this task
none the model found nothing unrecorded
nothing new nothing written to the transcript since the last sweep (no model call)
skipped: <reason> off (kill switch, WB_CAPTURE_SWEEP or sweep=off), muted, no session, no transcript, no jq
failed: <reason> timeout, error (model or shelving failed) or invalid (the model broke the output contract)

It does not wait on an async sweep. If the two overlap, the worst case is a duplicate draft, which the review dismisses in one step.

Stale drafts. The sweep snapshots the task's ## Decisions section (the exact text the model is shown), then waits up to a minute for the model. Edits elsewhere in the task file don't count. If ## Decisions changed in that window (usually /wb-decide recording the very decision being drafted), the answer is thrown away and the model is asked once more against the current section, so it either answers NONE or drafts something genuinely new (sweep_ran logs rerun: true). Only if the section changes again during the rerun is the draft shelved but flagged stale (a .stale sidecar file beside it): the review shows it next to the newest ## Decisions entries and asks whether it is already recorded. Events about a stale draft carry "stale":true.

Closed tasks and no session. A shelved draft whose task is already closed is still resolvable from any wb session, or from none: path-carrying accept, dismiss and keep ignore the kill switch and need no @task. The task stem comes from --task <stem>, else the session's task, else the command exits 1 with a message. With no session the event is logged with an empty session_id. wb-capture.sh shelved --task <stem> lists one task's drafts and shelved --all lists every task's, grouped by task. Drafts left by wb done --leave-shelved are reachable this way.

wb done won't strand decision drafts. wb done refuses (before it touches anything, including a store-only close) while the task's shelf holds any draft. It asks wb-capture.sh pending-for-task rather than reading the state dir itself, prints each path as shelved: <path>, and points to /close-out or /wb-decide shelved, or to re-running with wb done --leave-shelved to close anyway. --leave-shelved closes, logs one shelved_left{count} line to the capture log (via wb-capture.sh log-shelved-left) and notes the left drafts in the task's wb done Handoffs entry. A wb done invoked through /wb-done relays the refusal; it never adds --leave-shelved on its own.

Resolved drafts ~/.local/state/wb/capture/resolved/<task-stem>/<timestamp>-<outcome>-<name>.md, where <outcome> is shelved_accepted or shelved_dismissed and <name> is the shelved file's name (kept 30 days, then pruned by the hourly reaper; to recover one, copy it back into shelved/<task>/)
Scripts ~/.config/scripts/tmux/wb-capture.sh (verbs: hook-prompt, hook-stop, hook-end, hook-posttool (a silent no-op kept for sessions started before the change); accept|dismiss|keep [--task <stem>] <shelved path>; sweep; mute, unmute, status; store verbs that need no session: shelved [--all [--min-age-days <N>] | --task <stem> | --count --task <stem>], pending-for-task <task-file>, log-shelved-left <stem> <count>); the sweep itself is wb-capture-sweep.sh; the status-bar segment is capture-status.sh

The hooks do a few small jobs: hook-prompt and hook-stop record the session's transcript path (the checkpoint sweep reads it from there), hook-stop also records the task path and counts quiet turns, hook-end marks the session ended, and an hourly reaper tidies old session dirs, moves any leftover pre-change per-session draft to the shelf (reason reaped) and prunes resolved/.

Config keys — KEY=value lines in capture.conf, parsed line by line (never sourced), unknown keys and bad values ignored/defaulted:

Key Default Meaning
sweep_every 4 unchanged turns before an async sweep
sweep on on / off — the background model sweep (a checkpoint sweep then prints skipped: off)
sweep_timeout 60 seconds for the sweep child (0 means the default 60; the reaper's grace for an ended session, 2 x timeout + 60s, uses the same fallback)
sweep_max_failures 3 consecutive failed sweeps before the async sweep stops for the session (0 = never stop)
sweep_model sonnet model alias or id for the sweep child (letters, digits, ., _, -, not starting with -; anything else falls back to the default). Measured 2026-10-22 on a sweep-sized call: Haiku about $0.006, Sonnet 5.5 at medium effort about $0.124, both about 2s
sweep_effort medium low / medium / high / xhigh / max — the child's --effort (anything else falls back to the default)

Log events (log.jsonl, one JSON object per line: ts, session_id, task, event, plus event fields). Every event about one drafted decision carries a draft field holding the shelf file's name, which is how a decision is followed from shelving to review:

Event Fields Logged when
draft_shelved reason (sweep, or reaped for a leftover pre-change draft), draft, stale: true when stale a drafted decision is written to the shelf (once per file)
shelved_accepted draft, stale: true when stale accept moved a draft to resolved/
shelved_dismissed draft, stale: true when stale dismiss moved a draft to resolved/
shelved_kept draft keep — a review showed the draft and you left it; the file stays
shelved_left count (empty session_id) wb done --leave-shelved closed a task with drafts still shelved
sweep_ran result (none, draft, invalid, error, timeout), model, effort; entries on a draft (1-3: how many files it became); rerun: true; stale: true a sweep's model call finished. invalid means an answer that is neither NONE nor a ### entry: its excerpt is retried once, then skipped, and it never counts toward sweep_max_failures
sweep_skipped reason: disabled, muted, backoff, no_new_content a sweep ended without a model call
muted / unmuted none /wb-decide mute / unmute

Events from before the 2026-10-24 change (nudge_fired, followed, ignored, dismissed, pending_at_end, pending_orphaned) stay in old logs but nothing writes them now. draft_shelved, shelved_accepted and shelved_dismissed lines from before it have no draft field, which is how the queries below tell old drafts from new ones.

Measuring it — three measures, over drafts shelved after the 2026-10-24 rollout only (a draft with no draft field is older and is left out). They match the graduation review's gates:

  • Accuracy — accepted out of accepted + dismissed. It says whether drafts are right. A draft you chose to leave counts for neither side, because leaving is often legitimate (not decided yet, waiting on something outside the session). The review's gate is at least 70% over at least 10 resolved drafts.
  • Delivery — every draft is shown at a review (a shelved_kept, shelved_accepted or shelved_dismissed event) within 7 days of being shelved. It says whether drafts actually reach you. A draft nobody showed you for a week is a failure of the checkpoints, not of the model.
  • Left 3+ times — drafts shown at 3 or more reviews and still on the shelf. Reported, not gated: probably not real decisions, or review fatigue. The weekly review offers them for dismissal.
jq -s --argjson now "$(date +%s)" '
def epoch: capture("^(?<t>.*)(?<s>[+-])(?<h>[0-9]{2}):(?<m>[0-9]{2})$")
  | ((.t + "Z") | fromdateiso8601)
    - (if .s == "+" then 1 else -1 end) * ((.h | tonumber) * 3600 + (.m | tonumber) * 60);
def pct($a;$b): if $b == 0 then null else (($a * 1000 / $b | round) / 10) end;
def has_draft: ((.draft // "") != "");
[ .[] | select(.event == "draft_shelved" and has_draft) ] as $shelved
| [ .[] | select(.event | IN("shelved_kept","shelved_accepted","shelved_dismissed"))
          | select(has_draft) ] as $reviews
| ( $shelved | map( . as $s
    | ($reviews | map(select(.draft == $s.draft))) as $r
    | { draft: $s.draft, task: $s.task, shelved_at: ($s.ts | epoch),
        first_shown_at: ($r | map(.ts | epoch) | min),
        kept: ($r | map(select(.event == "shelved_kept")) | length),
        outcome: ( if   any($r[]; .event == "shelved_accepted")  then "accepted"
                   elif any($r[]; .event == "shelved_dismissed") then "dismissed"
                   else "waiting" end ) } ) ) as $d
| ($d | map(select(.outcome == "accepted"))  | length) as $acc
| ($d | map(select(.outcome == "dismissed")) | length) as $dis
| 604800 as $week
| {
    shelved_since_rollout: ($d | length),
    accuracy: { accepted: $acc, dismissed: $dis, resolved: ($acc + $dis),
                accuracy_pct: pct($acc; $acc + $dis),
                enough_sample: (($acc + $dis) >= 10) },
    delivery: {
      shown_within_7d: ($d | map(select(.first_shown_at != null and (.first_shown_at - .shelved_at) <= $week)) | length),
      shown_late:      ($d | map(select(.first_shown_at != null and (.first_shown_at - .shelved_at) >  $week)) | length),
      never_shown_over_7d: ($d | map(select(.first_shown_at == null and ($now - .shelved_at) > $week)) | length),
      waiting_under_7d:    ($d | map(select(.first_shown_at == null and ($now - .shelved_at) <= $week)) | length) },
    left_3_plus_times: ($d | map(select(.outcome == "waiting" and .kept >= 3) | {task, draft, kept}))
  }' ~/.local/state/wb/capture/log.jsonl

Reading it: the delivery gate passes when shown_late and never_shown_over_7d are both 0. The accuracy gate passes when enough_sample is true and accuracy_pct is 70 or more. Fewer than 10 resolved drafts by the review date moves the review out a week.

Close-out interaction. /close-out step 4 runs the same checkpoint review (sweep, then each draft) before the wind-down. If ## Decisions is still empty after it, the agent either writes a real entry or appends an affirmed marker (### <timestamp> — close-out / _No decisions: <reason>_) via wb append. An unmarked empty section keeps meaning "nobody checked". Tasks closed with bare wb done (no /close-out) are left unmarked.

/wb-save interaction. /wb-save runs the review before composing its entry, so the Handoffs entry can say how many decisions were recorded, dismissed and left.

Rollout. Stow (stow --no-folding -t "$HOME" claude scripts tmux from the main checkout after fast-forwarding) and restart Claude sessions. The plugin loads automatically as wb-capture@skills-dir. Already-running sessions keep calling the old hook-posttool registration (a silent no-op) until restarted; editing the scripts takes effect live, but adding or removing a hook entry needs a restart. A draft left in the old per-session slot is moved to the shelf once by hand at rollout; nothing writes that slot any more.

Known rough edges

  • Only works inside a wb tmux session with @task set; elsewhere the agent verbs are silent no-ops (the store verbs and path-carrying accept, dismiss, keep work anywhere).
  • A draft sees only a transcript excerpt, so it can be wrong or already recorded. Accuracy (above) measures how often.
  • A checkpoint sweep and an async sweep don't lock against each other: the worst case is a duplicate draft (dismissed in one step) or a draft that lands just after a review (it stays on the shelf and in the count).
  • The status count follows the current session's task, not a total across tasks. wb board shows every task's count.
  • /clear and wb resume session-id behaviour is still being confirmed live.

Next steps / reverting

A graduation review (dotfiles--capture-graduation-review, due 2026-11-02) decides, from the accuracy, delivery and left-3+-times figures above, whether to keep the feature, retune it, or drop it. It also gates on no task closing after rollout with an unmarked empty ## Decisions.

To turn it off, in order of bluntness:

  • Kill switch (all sessions, instant): touch ~/.local/state/wb/disable-capture; rm the file to re-enable. (Resolving a shelved draft by path or --task still works with it on: that is cleanup of existing drafts, not capture.)
  • Sweep only: sweep=off in ~/.config/wb/capture.conf.
  • One session: /wb-decide mute (and /wb-decide unmute).
  • Removal: delete the plugin dir and restart sessions.
